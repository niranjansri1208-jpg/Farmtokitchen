How FarmTrace Works

FarmTrace is an offline agricultural traceability platform that gives vegetable harvests a unique verifiable identity, tracking cold chain milestones from field to table.

1. TRACE

Unique cryptographic batch codes (e.g. FT-2026-000123) and high-contrast offline QR labels.

2. TRACK

Authorized handlers log temperature, humidity, transit distances, and consignment checkpoints.

3. DISCOVER

Shoppers scan crate QR codes with any smartphone camera to view the verified harvest story.
